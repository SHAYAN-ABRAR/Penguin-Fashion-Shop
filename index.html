        04 / THE DETAILS

        Good to know

        Short answers about sizing, your bag and how this shop works.

        How do I find my size?

        Every piece has a size guide with step-by-step measuring instructions. Measure your chest, waist and sleeve, then choose the size that fits your chest. If you're between sizes, or you'll wear chunky knitwear underneath, choose the larger one.

        Is my bag saved?

        Yes, on this device. Your bag and saved pieces are kept in this browser's local storage, so they're still here after you refresh or come back later. They aren't sent anywhere, they don't sync to your other devices, and clearing this site's data empties them.

        Can I place an order?

        No. Penguin Fashion is a demo storefront: the prices are sample values, there's no checkout, and nothing is charged or shipped. Use the bag to plan a look and compare pieces.

        How do I save a piece for later?

        Select the heart on any piece. Your saved pieces are one tap away from the heart at the top of the page, and they stay on this device.

        How should I look after a winter jacket?

        Always follow the care label inside the garment. In general, hang coats on a broad hanger, let wet jackets dry at room temperature away from direct heat, and store leather and puffers somewhere cool and dry with space around them.

        What is the Winter Edit?

        Three looks put together from this collection: everyday layers, bold color and weekend weather. Each one has a styling idea and links to its pieces, and Shop this edit shows just those pieces in the collection.
