# Campaign imagery

These three images were created for Penguin Fashion with the built-in image generation tool. They are styling inspiration, not photographs of the catalog products. The storefront labels them accordingly and retains the original product photos in shopping views.

The web assets are self-hosted WebP files. Each has two responsive widths; no external image service is required. The source PNGs were resized without cropping and exported at quality 84.

## city

Files: `city-768.webp`, `city-1536.webp`.

Leave the LEFT 45 percent as open softly shadowed concrete wall and pavement for white website typography. Camera low but not exaggerated, contemporary European fashion magazine campaign, subtle analogue grain, tactile fabrics, natural imperfect skin, cinematic yet believable winter daylight, striking ochre against cool grey. No text, no lettering, no logos, no watermark, no collage. Do not depict an actual brand's garment. This is mood photography, not a product catalog.
